Our Work

🧩 Real-World Impact: AHV International

At AHV International, I designed and delivered end-to-end automation tools that dramatically improved operational efficiency and decision-making across the business.

📝 Automated Report Generation System

Designed a powerful reporting pipeline that reduced manual work from days to seconds. Leveraged Python, Excel automation, and branded templates to ensure consistent, high-quality output. Reports supported multi-million pound business decisions in agriculture and animal health.

🧠 Data Analysis & Strategic Insights

Built systems to extract, clean, and analyse large datasets across multiple sources — including internal spreadsheets and third-party portals. Delivered actionable insights on milk quality, fertility trends, and herd health, crucial for consultants and business leaders.

🌐 Custom Web Scraping Tools

Developed secure scripts to automatically fetch critical data from external sites (e.g. CIS, Herd Companion), saving dozens of hours weekly and eliminating human error.

🖥️ User-Friendly Application for Non-Technical Staff

Engineered a GUI-based app allowing staff to generate tailored reports with zero coding or Excel knowledge. Designed for use across multiple locations — intuitive for users, yet powered by a complex automation backend.

💼 Digital Transformation & Workflow Automation

Replaced fragmented manual processes with seamless digital workflows, integrating file downloads, email scraping, spreadsheet parsing, PDF generation, and commentary writing using AI.

💡 Key Outcomes:

  • Hundreds of hours saved monthly
  • Improved accuracy and consistency
  • Faster decision-making
  • Tools used across departments, enabling scalable growth

🏥 Digital Revolution: The Gatehouse Vets

I successfully spearheaded a transformative project, revolutionizing a paper-based system for a veterinary surgery into a cutting-edge digital solution. Through comprehensive stakeholder engagement and iterative development, I delivered a complete practice management system.

📋 Complete Practice Management System

Designed and built an intuitive booking system with streamlined mechanisms for adding new staff and customers to a centralized database. Implemented diverse search and sort algorithms for efficient client management and appointment scheduling.

💰 Advanced Cost Calculation & Billing

Developed an intricate cost calculation feature for precise procedure costing, enabling accurate billing and improved financial tracking for the veterinary practice.

📦 Integrated Stock Management

Created a comprehensive stock tracking database with seamless online stock ordering capabilities, ensuring optimal inventory levels and reducing manual stock management overhead.

🔐 Secure Access & User Management

Implemented a secure password-protected login interface with password recovery mechanism via email, plus specialized admin access for managing staff permissions and time-off requests.

📅 Dynamic Calendar & Scheduling

Built a dynamic calendar synchronized with appointments for enhanced organization, including provisions for requesting and approving time off within the system, streamlining practice operations.

💡 Key Outcomes:

  • Complete digitization of paper-based processes
  • Streamlined appointment and staff management
  • Improved accuracy in billing and stock control
  • Enhanced operational efficiency and user satisfaction
